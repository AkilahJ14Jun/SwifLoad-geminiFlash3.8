Need to build a fully modular on-demand logistics mobile app solution for Android and iOS.

Build a **Starter MVP** for a single-city on-demand goods-transport marketplace. The scope should cover three primary roles: **customer/shipper, driver-partner, and operations admin**—with fleet-owner and business-account functions added during growth. This app’s public offering similarly spans consumer booking, driver earnings/trip management, and enterprise logistics controls. 

## Starter MVP

### Customer app

- Mobile-number login with OTP and basic profile management
- Select pickup and drop address through map search, pin placement, and saved addresses
- Choose vehicle category, such as two-wheeler, three-wheeler, mini-truck, or pickup
- Enter shipment details: goods category, approximate weight/quantity, optional photo, loading/unloading requirement, and recipient contact
- Instant or rule-based fare estimate based on distance, vehicle type, demand, and serviceability zone
- Schedule “now” bookings; optionally support scheduled bookings also
- Booking confirmation with driver/vehicle details after allocation
- Real-time driver location and trip-status timeline: driver assigned, arriving, pickup, in transit, delivered, cancelled
- One-tap call or masked-call connection with the driver
- Digital payment via UPI/payment gateway, GPay, PhonePe, Netbanking (only IMPS), plus cash-on-delivery options
- Booking history, invoice/receipt, and trip details
- Cancellation flow with configurable cancellation charges
- Ratings, feedback, and basic help/support ticket or WhatsApp support link
- Push notifications and SMS/WhatsApp fallback for booking, assignment, pickup, delivery, and cancellation events

### Driver-partner app

- OTP-based login and driver onboarding application
- Upload KYC and vehicle documents: licence, RC, insurance, bank/UPI payout details, and vehicle photos
- Admin approval/rejection of driver and vehicle onboarding
- Online/offline availability toggle
- Nearby-trip request screen showing pickup/drop locations, distance, estimated earnings, vehicle requirement, and expected route
- Accept, reject, or timeout trip offers
- In-app navigation deep links to Google Maps
- Trip workflow: arrive at pickup, start trip, reach destination, complete delivery
- OTP or delivery confirmation at pickup/drop; photo proof can be included if required
- Customer calling/messaging through a privacy-safe mechanism
- Daily and trip-level earnings summary, completed-trip history, and pending payout amount
- Cancellation/no-show reason capture
- In-app support contact and basic issue reporting

Driver transparency is particularly important: The driver-partner should publicly highlight upfront trip distance/earnings, order history, cancellation updates, payouts, and support. 

### Operations admin portal

- Admin login with role-based access: super admin, dispatcher, support, finance
- Live bookings board with filters by status, area, vehicle type, and assigned driver
- Driver and vehicle onboarding/approval management
- Manual dispatch and driver reassignment
- Pricing configuration: base fare, per-km rate, waiting/loading fee, surge multiplier, cancellation fee, service zones
- Serviceability configuration by city, pincode, zone, or geo-fence
- Live driver map with online/offline and trip status
- Customer, driver, trip, payment, and cancellation records
- Basic dashboard: total bookings, completed trips, cancellations, GMV, driver earnings, and platform commission
- Refund, adjustment, and cash-collection tracking
- Support tools for viewing a trip timeline and adding internal notes
- CSV export for trips, users, payouts, and finance reconciliation
- Audit trail for pricing changes, trip reassignment, refunds, and status overrides

### MVP platform essentials

- Backend APIs, database, API documentation, and secure authentication
- GPS/location streaming with battery-conscious update intervals
- Payment-gateway integration, e.g., via UPI,GPay/Razorpay/PhonePe,Netbanking (only IMPS) gateway
- Maps, routing, distance matrix, geocoding, and geofencing integration
- Notification stack: FCM, SMS/OTP, optional WhatsApp
- Centralized logs, error monitoring, backups, and basic operational alerts
- Consent, privacy policy, terms, data retention policy, and secure handling of KYC documents
- Android-first customer and driver apps plus a responsive web admin portal
